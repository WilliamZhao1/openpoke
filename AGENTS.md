# Tracking system limitations

Maintain `docs/known-limitations.md` as the central register of known OpenPoke limitations.

- When repository work or a user discussion identifies, changes, or resolves a system limitation, update the relevant entry as part of that work.
- Reuse stable IDs. Give new entries concrete behavior/impact, existing safeguards, evidence links, status, and a condition for reconsidering or closing the item.
- Separate source-confirmed behavior, intentional bounds, deferred scope, and evidence gaps. Do not present speculation or scripted/mock results as established production failures or live quality evidence.
- Preserve resolved entries with the fix date and validation reference. Do not mark a broader limitation resolved because only one observed case was fixed.
- Keep historical comparison reports intact. Link to their evidence instead of rewriting old results.
- Tracking a limitation does not authorize implementing a deferred feature or expanding the user's current task.
